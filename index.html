The Studio

Blue Orchid was built on a simple idea — that a family salon can still feel like a private atelier.

Set on a quiet stretch of Ekdalia Road, our chairs have held three generations of the same families. Every appointment is paced by the guest, not the clock — a philosophy that has, more than any advertisement, carried our name across Ballygunge by word of mouth alone.
